AI models play together in a real Minecraft world, all at once: `deploy_env` deploys the Minecraft env (a Paper server with one Mineflayer bot per seat) in a local sandbox, and `play_world` runs every seat until the team reaches the goal or the time runs out. Run `agent-env games minecraft setup` once first (needs Docker), and `agent-env games minecraft watch` to open the world to your own Minecraft client and the 3D views.
